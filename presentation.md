## Slide 4: Case 1: AePS Biometric Cloning Fraud
*   **Modus Operandi:** Scraped land records -> Silicone fingerprint printing -> Cash withdrawal from bank via micro-ATMs without OTP.
*   **Legal Mappings:** IT Act Section 66C (Identity Theft) & Sec 43. DPDP Act Sec 8 (Data Security Breach by Registries). BNS Sec 318 (Cheating).
*   **Ethical Issue:** Breach of digital bodily privacy (fingerprints are permanent).
*   **Solution:** Lock Aadhaar biometrics via mAadhaar. Mask registries.
*   **Visual Suggestion:** Step-by-step flowchart showing the lifecycle of the fingerprint from public registry to silicone clone to bank withdrawal.

## Slide 5: Case 2: Digital Arrest & Extortion Scam
*   **Modus Operandi:** FedEx/Customs parcel scare call -> Transferred to fake IPS/CBI officer -> Kept under Skype video confinement -> Forced bank transfers.
*   **Legal Mappings:** IT Act Section 66D (Cheating by Personation). BNS Sec 308 (Extortion) & Sec 204 (Impersonating Public Servant).
*   **Ethical Issue:** Extreme psychological intimidation and violation of liberty.
*   **Solution:** Disconnect video calls claiming arrest. Report details to 1930.
*   **Visual Suggestion:** Icon showing a laptop lock and Skype logo inside a warning triangle.

## Slide 6: Case 3: Telegram Task & Part-Time Job Scam
*   **Modus Operandi:** Social media work-from-home offers -> Paid ₹150 for small likes/ratings (trust-building) -> Demanded deposits for VIP commissions -> Locked funds.
*   **Legal Mappings:** IT Act Section 66D. BNS Sec 318 & Sec 61 (Criminal Conspiracy).
*   **Ethical Issue:** Behavioral grooming and exploitation of economic vulnerability.
*   **Solution:** Reject jobs demanding payment. Verify recruiter authenticity.
*   **Visual Suggestion:** A balance scale: Left showing trust-building (small UPI payouts), Right showing exploitation (large deposits locked).

## Slide 7: Case 4: SEBI-Impersonating Trading Scam
*   **Modus Operandi:** WhatsApp/Telegram trading groups -> Fake SEBI licenses -> Unofficial APK apps showing fake simulated profits -> Blocked withdrawals.
*   **Legal Mappings:** IT Act Section 66C & 66D. BNS Sec 336/340 (Forgery). SEBI Act Sec 12 (Unregistered Advisory).
*   **Ethical Issue:** Dark patterns (deceptive UI dashboards showing fake profits).
*   **Solution:** Check registrations on sebi.gov.in. Never install third-party APKs.
*   **Visual Suggestion:** Screenshot comparison of a fake trading dashboard (simulated gains) vs. actual registered stock portals.

## Slide 8: Case 5: Electricity Bill KYC & Screen Hijack
*   **Modus Operandi:** SMS warning of power cut -> Instructed to install AnyDesk/TeamViewer to update KYC -> OTP read from screen -> Bank accounts drained.
*   **Legal Mappings:** IT Act Section 43 (System Breach) & Sec 66C. BNS Sec 318.
*   **Ethical Issue:** Exploit utility dependency to induce panic. Target non-tech-savvy.
*   **Solution:** Never share remote control access. Verify bills directly on utility portal.
*   **Visual Suggestion:** Red alert icon showing mobile device screen with remote control cursor, highlighting the leakage of banking credentials.

## Slide 10: Ethical & Legal Guidelines for Developers
*   **Privacy by Design:** Integrate user-consent parameters under DPDP Act 2023.
*   **Deceptive Patterns:** Prohibit dark patterns that mislead financial expectations.
*   **Secure Storage:** Localize biometric signatures in hardware secure enclaves.
*   **Security Auditing:** Regular pen-testing of registry databases and bank portals.
