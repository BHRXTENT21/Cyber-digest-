Project Scope & Objectives

This academic project implements a comprehensive threat intelligence and ethical evaluation framework. By focusing on recent real-world vectors documented by the MHA Cyber Dost initiative, it aims to:

  • Deconstruct Modus Operandi: Analyze how social engineering, identity cloning, and technical exploits bypass defense perimeters.
  • Map Cyber Jurisprudence: Align modern incidents with specific punitive provisions under the Information Technology Act, 2000, Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023, and data protections under the DPDP Act, 2023.
  • Evaluate Digital Ethics: Analyze systemic failures in data custody, violations of bodily and behavioral privacy, and coercive psychological manipulation.
  • Recommend Responsible Tech: Formulate specific preventive policies for developers, institutions, and individual citizens.
